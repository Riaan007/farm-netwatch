Radios

Every radio Netwatch reads over SSH.

Reading the numbers

The same limits the alerts use.

Signal
How loud the other radio arrives. −65 dBm or better is good, −75 is weak, −82 is poor. A drop of 6 dB from a link's own normal matters more than the number itself.
Link score
airMAX's 0–100 rating of the data rate actually achieved. 80+ good, below 50 poor, below 35 bad. Strong signal with a low score means interference or a partly blocked path, not aim.
Download / upload
Download = access point to station, upload = station to access point. The weak direction points at the end that hears interference.
Air time
How much of the channel is in use. Above 80 % the link is congested — usually camera streams.
Noise floor
Background radio noise. Around −90 dBm is quiet; a rise of 8 dB means something new is transmitting nearby.
Antenna chains
A dish receives on two polarisations. They should be within 3–4 dB; 8 dB apart means a mis-seated radio, a twisted dish or water in the feed.
Fresnel zone
The oval around the line between two radios that must stay clear. Trees inside it keep the signal strong but ruin the link score.

Uptime Kuma

Up/down monitoring and uptime graphs for the devices you pick.

Open Kuma ↗

Run it with docker compose --profile kuma up -d, create the Kuma admin account, and enter it above. From then on Kuma follows the Monitored list: every monitored device gets a 60-second ping monitor (tagged with its category, and following the device when DHCP moves it); a device taken off the list has its monitor paused, history kept.

Network

Extra IP addresses for other subnets, and DHCP or a fixed IP for this Pi.

Managed extra IPs (multi-subnet)

Adds a second IP so the Pi sits on another LAN and ARP-scans it. Additive — never touches your main connection. Re-applied on reboot.

DHCP ↔ Fixed IP

⚠️ A wrong fixed IP/gateway can cut this Pi off. Changes auto-revert to DHCP unless you confirm — on a remote site, confirm only after you've checked the dashboard still loads.

Fixed-IP control needs the NetworkManager image (docker-compose.netcfg.yml) — not enabled on this host.

Wireless bridges

Radios that answer for the devices behind them (proxy-ARP).

Devices behind these MACs are tracked by IP so each one shows separately. Once you fix the bridge itself (e.g. turn off proxy-ARP), remove its MAC here to go back to normal tracking — the devices re-merge on the next scan. Add one from a "Same MAC, multiple IPs" problem on the Devices page.

Backup & restore

Move this site to a new Pi, or undo a bad change.

Everything needed to rebuild this site on a new Pi: settings, device names and categories, saved logins and the hub-VPN link. The central hub also takes an encrypted backup every day. Treat a downloaded file like a password — it is not encrypted.

🧹

Maintenance

Keep the device list tidy.

Forget offline devices not seen for days

Removes stale devices (visitors' phones, kit from another network, retired gear) from the list, registry and uptime history. 0 days removes every device that is offline right now. Monitored devices are never removed here — take them off the Monitored list first, or Forget them one at a time. Anything still on the network comes back on the next scan. Event history is kept.
